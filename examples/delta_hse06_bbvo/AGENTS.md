# BBVO project instructions

## Scientific direction — Lavrenty, 2026-09-30

Imaginary harmonic phonon modes, symmetry-breaking relaxation, or failure to remove all soft modes are **not standalone blockers and not standalone arguments against BBVO**. Do not conclude that BBVO is unreal, unusable, unsynthesizable, or unpublishable from these observations alone. Do not require an all-real 0 K phonon spectrum before continuing the electronic-structure, conditional device, or MLIP work.

The user's motivation is the experience of halide-perovskite technology: idealized harmonic/phase stability is not by itself a practical viability criterion. This analogy motivates keeping possibilities open; it does not prove a specific stabilization mechanism for BBVO. Distinguish the 0 K harmonic reference from finite-temperature, anharmonic, strained, interfacial, composition-dependent and kinetically retained states. Report what has actually been demonstrated.

### Stabilization is an optional, bounded opportunity
Pursue stabilization only where a plausible, accessible route can be screened at modest computational and experimental cost (for example limited substitution or realistic strain). Here “low barrier to pursue” means practical feasibility and cost, **not** a requirement for a low transformation activation energy. Agree a bounded screening budget before expansion. If no inexpensive promising route is found, defer stabilization and continue the core project; do not abandon BBVO, a dopant with other benefits, or the device direction solely because soft modes remain. Do not turn a full finite-temperature stabilization proof into a mandatory new campaign.

Phonons, mode scans and NEB are diagnostic tools. An unstable starting structure may call for downhill mode following instead of a barrier calculation. Neither result is a project pass/fail criterion.

### Keep independent evidence and validity requirements separate
Preserve and assess competing-phase thermodynamics, kinetic accessibility, experimental phase identification, operating conditions and electronic/transport assumptions independently. A lower 0 K polymorph or positive hull energy does not alone establish experimental inaccessibility. Imaginary modes do not establish decomposition. Do not replace the target phase's device inputs automatically with those of the lowest 0 K structure; label phase-specific predictions and their operating-condition assumptions.

This direction does not validate an unsupported efficiency claim, waive missing SCAPS provenance, override a band-gap-dependent physical bound, or excuse an inaccurate MLIP. Model applicability, force/stress accuracy and training coverage remain gates for trustworthy MLIP calculations. Gate readiness on the model describing the relevant physics, not on the material having no imaginary modes.

### Precedence and dispatch
This dated project direction supersedes earlier audit prose, stopping rules and assignments wherever they make harmonic phonon stability or successful stabilization a prerequisite for continuing BBVO. Preserve numerical findings and provenance; revise their interpretation. Prepared diagnostic packages may remain useful, but do not create a new mandatory stabilization gate, cancel unrelated jobs, or launch an expanded campaign from this instruction alone. Carry this direction into smoke-dispatch handoffs.

## Dopant objective hierarchy — explicit PI clarification, 2026-09-30

The **primary purpose of Nb/Ta substitution is improved CBM dispersion**, assessed with electronic-structure calculations (band character, dispersion/curvature and appropriate effective-mass tensors at consistently treated geometries). Preserve useful gap and band-alignment properties and report tradeoffs. An MLIP can supply geometries and structural sampling but does not predict CBM dispersion. A lighter band mass alone does not establish a measured mobility or exclude localization.

Suppression of imaginary phonon modes is a **secondary, optional objective**. Rank and advance dopants primarily by CBM/electronic-property improvement, not by removal of a rattle drop or soft mode. Retain promising CBM candidates even when imaginary modes remain. A dopant that stabilizes a structure but does not improve the CBM has not met the primary objective. Defer an unproductive stabilization search without dropping the electronic screening. Dispatch handoffs must preserve this ordering and must not gate dopant electronic calculations on a stable harmonic spectrum.
